Getting started

What is QEA Master?

A viewer for model repositories stored in .qea and .qeax files, such as those saved by Sparx Systems Enterprise Architect. It opens a repository and draws its UML®, BPMN™ and ArchiMate® diagrams natively, so you can walk through a model in a meeting, on the go or at your desk. The app is written natively for Apple platforms: there is no web view inside it, and it needs no account and no internet connection.

How do I open a repository?

On iPhone and iPad, launch QEA Master and choose the file in the document browser — in Recents, iCloud Drive or On My iPhone (or iPad) → QEA Master — or tap the file in the Files app. On Mac, double-click the file in Finder, or choose File → Open. The repository opens on its first diagram.

Which files does it open?

  • .qea — a file-based repository with an SQLite database inside.
  • .qeax — the same for shared use, including repositories in WAL mode.

The format is recognised from the content, not the extension. Projects kept in other file formats or on a server are not opened: save or transfer the project to a .qea or .qeax file in your modelling tool first.

Exploring a repository

How do I move around a diagram?

Drag anywhere to pan, and pinch to zoom; on Mac, scroll to move and hold ⌘ while scrolling to zoom. The zoom controls in the bottom-right corner have − and +, a fit button, and preset zoom levels behind the percentage. The minimap in the corner shows the whole diagram — drag inside it to move. The “…” menu has Fit to window, Zoom to Selection and switches for the grid and the minimap.

Click or tap a shape to select it. Double-click or double-tap a package, a diagram frame or a composite element to open its diagram; any other shape is zoomed to.

How do I switch between diagrams?

The diagram switcher at the top of the canvas lists every diagram by package. On iPad and Mac, the Diagrams tab of the sidebar lists them too. The back and forward arrows beside the diagram switcher walk through the diagrams you opened (⌘[ and ⌘] with a keyboard).

Where do I see notes, tagged values and connectors?

In Properties, the inspector. On iPad and Mac it is the panel on the right, shown and hidden with the right-hand panel button in the toolbar; on iPhone, the same button opens it as a sheet. For a selected element it shows:

  • its type, stereotype and profile, alias, status, author, version, package, owner, classifier and GUID;
  • its notes, tagged values, attributes and operations;
  • every incoming and outgoing connector — tap one to go to the element at its other end;
  • every diagram it appears on — tap one to open that diagram with the element selected.

A connector shows its ends, type, stereotype, direction, roles and multiplicities. A package shows its contents and its first diagram, and with nothing selected Properties describes the diagram and the repository.

How do I browse the whole repository?

Open the Model Browser with the left-hand panel button in the toolbar: on iPad and Mac it is the sidebar, on iPhone a sheet. Model is the repository tree — packages, subpackages, diagrams and elements with their nested elements and diagrams, each with a miniature of its figure. Diagrams lists every diagram by package. The field at the top filters by name and keeps the path to each match open. Selecting a shape on the canvas highlights it in the tree.

How do I find an element?

Find Element (⌘F) finds shapes on the current diagram by name, alias or GUID and steps through them in reading order. The command palette (⌘K) searches the whole repository — every diagram, package, element and connector — and jumps to the result, opening the first diagram that shows it when it is not on the current one. It also runs any command. On iPhone and iPad both are in the “…” menu.

Are there keyboard shortcuts?

Yes, on Mac and on iPad with a keyboard: ⌘K command palette, ⌘F find, ⌘[ and ⌘] back and forward, ⌘+ and ⌘− zoom, ⌘0 actual size, ⌘9 fit to window, ⌘3 zoom to selection, ⇧⌘E export as PNG and ⌘P print. The full list is on the Keyboard page in Settings.

Files and sharing

How do I export or print a diagram?

On iPhone and iPad, open Export in the “…” menu; on Mac, use the Export button in the toolbar or File → Export. You can export the current diagram as PNG or as a vector PDF with selectable text, or every diagram as one PDF with a page per diagram. Print is next to them (⌘P). Exports and printouts always use the light paper look, without the selection, whichever theme you use on screen.

Why do the colours look different from my modelling tool?

Shapes are filled with the colours of the app's theme, by kind — classes, behaviour, structure, ArchiMate layers — tuned to its warm palette. Colours someone set on purpose in the repository, such as a red line or a highlighted box, are kept. To ignore the file's colours entirely, turn off Use colours from the file in Settings → Canvas.

A file does not open. Why?

QEA Master checks what the file actually contains and says so: a text shortcut to a server-based repository, a different database, an empty or damaged file. Server-based repositories are not supported — export the project to a .qea or .qeax file to view it. If a file that opens in your modelling tool fails here, please send it to us.

What is not shown yet?

Images and alternate images stored in a repository, linked documents, diagram legends and custom shape appearances. Shapes affected are drawn in their standard notation; the rest of the repository opens as usual.

Can I edit a model?

No. QEA Master is a viewer: it opens and presents repositories and never changes the file. Edit the model in your modelling tool, save it, and open it again in QEA Master.

Where are my files stored?

Wherever you keep them. QEA Master uses the system file browser — Files on iPhone and iPad, Finder on Mac — including iCloud Drive and any other provider you have connected. On iPhone and iPad, the app's own folder is under Browse → On My iPhone (or iPad) → QEA Master.

Settings

Where is Settings, and what is in it?

On iPhone and iPad, in the “…” toolbar menu; on Mac, choose QEA Master → Settings or press ⌘,. It has four pages:

  • General — appearance and interface language
  • Canvas — dots, lines or a plain background, the minimap, colours from the file, shadows under shapes
  • Keyboard — every shortcut
  • About — version and build

How do I change the theme?

In Settings → General → Appearance. Terracotta, the default, uses the colours of the app icon; Light is the bright paper look; Dark is espresso with cream lines. The change applies at once.

Can I change the language?

Yes. The app is available in English, German, French, Ukrainian, Spanish, Japanese and Portuguese, with UML terms in the established words of each language. By default it follows your device language, falling back to English. To choose another, use Settings → General in the app, or system settings: Settings → Apps → QEA Master → Language on iPhone and iPad, System Settings → General → Language & Region → Applications on Mac. Both places change the same setting. The app's own screens switch at once; the file browser, menus and system dialogs follow the next time you open the app. Names, notes and tagged values from the model are always shown as written.

About the app

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The app works entirely offline, makes no network requests and collects no data. See the Privacy Policy.

Why did the app ask me for a rating?

Once you have used QEA Master for a while, it may show Apple's rating request right after something worked — an export, a print, or a few jumps to other diagrams of an element — at most once per version, and never right after something failed. Apple decides whether the request actually appears. To turn such requests off for all apps, go to Settings → App Store → In-App Ratings & Reviews on iPhone and iPad, or App Store → Settings on Mac.

System requirements

iPhone with iOS 18 or later, iPad with iPadOS 18 or later, or a Mac with macOS 15 or later.
